Thank you for considering contributing to this list!

There are no strict rules or complex automated checks here yet. If you know a useful tool, open-source project, specification, or guide that belongs here, feel free to share it. As this repository grows and becomes more valuable to the community, we may introduce more detailed guidelines later.

For now, just keep a few simple things in mind:

## Basic Guidelines

- **Keep it useful and relevant:** Share resources that genuinely help practitioners. Open-source repositories, free utilities, documentation, and high-signal technical guides are especially welcome.
- **Working, direct links only:** Please make sure the URL works (no broken links) and points directly to the project or resource. No affiliate, referral, or tracking links.
- **Short, objective descriptions:** Describe what the resource does in one concise, factual sentence. Avoid marketing fluff or promotional claims.
- **Avoid duplicates:** Do a quick search in `README.md` before adding a new entry to make sure it isn't already listed.
